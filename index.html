Turn a photo into a printable jigsaw.

Upload an image, pick a piece shape and count, fine-tune the color palette matched to your photo, and this generates a jigsaw as a color-tagged .3mf file — each piece pre-colored, laid out flat and separated, ready for your slicer.

Classic pieces have interlocking tabs, like a real jigsaw. Grid, triangle and hexagon pieces have straight edges and don't lock together.
The actual piece count may land a little above or below this, depending on the shape and your image's proportions.
03 — Colors
Colors are pulled from your photo automatically. Click any swatch to fine-tune it in your system color picker, or re-sync all of them below.
04 — Physical size
This is not the puzzle fit — the pieces still interlock exactly. It's the empty space left between pieces once they're laid out flat and separated on the print bed, so your slicer can tell them apart and you can pop them off cleanly after printing. Bigger nozzle or first-layer squish → use a bigger gap.
